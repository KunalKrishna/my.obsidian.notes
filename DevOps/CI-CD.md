## 8 phases of DevOps Lifecycle 

![[Pasted image 20260704014246.png]]


The DevOps lifecycle  : 

represents an evolution of more traditional software development approaches, such as waterfall and agile.

is a continuous, iterative process for software development and deployment, consisting of eight key phases: 
1. Plan: Setting the Foundation
2. Code: Writing with Purpose
3. Build: Bringing It All Together
4. Test: Ensuring Reliability
5. Release: Preparing for Deployment 
6. Deploy: Taking It Live
7. Operate: Keeping It Running Smoothly
8. monitor. 9. DevSecOps: The “ninth phase” - "security"
