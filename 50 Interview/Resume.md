Your resume is not getting rejected because you are not good.  
It’s getting rejected because it’s not clear.  
Recruiters spend 5–10 seconds on your resume.  
That’s it.  
  
Tell me honestly:  
Can someone understand your impact in 10 seconds?  
  
Most resumes fail here.  

Here’s what I see again and again:  
1. No impact  : “Worked on backend services”  → So what?    
2. No numbers  : No scale = no signal    
3. Too much detail  : Long paragraphs = skip  
4. No keywords  : No match → no interview  
5. No story  : Random experience = confusion  

After reviewing hundreds of resumes, one thing is clear:  
**Clarity** > complexity  
Fix this first.
